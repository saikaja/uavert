## Definition

### Goal
The news section shows each kind of event for as long as it's useful. It groups stories about the same event so the main ones stand out, and it shows break-in patterns from police reports. None of it changes a score.

### In scope
1. **A window per type of event:**

   | Type | Shown for | Examples |
   |---|---|---|
   | Protest | 2 days | rallies, marches, blockades |
   | Violent incident | 7 days | shootings, stabbings, robberies, assaults |
   | Property crime (new) | 60 days | break-ins, thefts, catalytic converters, arson |

2. **More sources:** CityNews and Global News, plus the Toronto Star if you approve it (see the decisions). They're collected hourly with CBC and GDELT. Each is a source with its own data date and collection time, and a failure in one doesn't affect the others.
3. **Better classification:**
   - sports results ("rally past", "champions") are skipped
   - stories about events in an earlier year ("in 2025", "memorial") are skipped
   - arson goes to property crime
   - the new property-crime type
4. **Trending:** stories about the same event, from the last 7 days, are grouped. A group is "trending" when it has at least 3 stories from at least 2 outlets within 3 days. It shows the newest headline, "N stories from K outlets since <date>", and links to each story.
5. **Patterns in police reports:** break-ins and auto thefts, by neighbourhood, using the rule above. They're recomputed with the daily score rebuild. Each is shown as "7 break-ins reported in Broadview North between Sep 2 and Oct 1, about 6× the usual for this area (police data up to Oct 1)". Clicking one opens that neighbourhood. A neighbourhood's own panel also shows its pattern, if it has one.
6. **The section's layout:** "News and patterns", with Trending, then Recent by type (each labelled with its window), then Patterns in police reports. News stays labelled unverified; patterns are labelled as police data with their date.

### Out of scope
- **Scores:** no change. Protest and violent-incident news keeps its existing, approved effect (it raises nearby blocks for 24 hours, at most to "elevated"); see decision 2. Property-crime news and police patterns affect nothing.
- **Reading full articles:** still headline, summary, link, publisher and time only.
- **Backfilling** past months of news: the feeds don't offer it.
- **Patterns at street level** (smaller than a neighbourhood). This is a follow-up.

### Acceptance criteria (added to criteria 1–35)
36. **Types and windows.** `classify()` returns `property_crime` for break-in, theft and arson headlines. Unit tests use the real headlines quoted above:
    - the Sixers cricket story and the 2025 memorial story return nothing
    - "Second Harvest theft" returns `property_crime`
    - the bike-lane protest stays `protest`
37. **Windows by type.** `/api/v1/news-events?window=by_type` returns protests from the last 2 days, violent incidents from the last 7 and property crime from the last 60, each with its type and window. Without `window`, it behaves as today (`since_hours`).
38. **New sources.** `ingest live` collects CityNews and Global (and the Star if approved). Each appears in `/api/v1/sources` with a data date and collection time. One feed failing leaves the others' stories stored, and is recorded as failed.
39. **Trending.** `/api/v1/news-trending` returns groups of at least 3 stories from at least 2 outlets within 3 days. A unit test with today's real headlines groups the 4 bike-lane stories together and the 5 Second Harvest stories together, without mixing them.
40. **Patterns.** `build-scores` stores police-report patterns using the rule above, and `/api/v1/crime-patterns` lists them with counts, the usual level, dates and the police data's date. Unit tests cover:
    - a spike that passes
    - a spike too small to pass (4 incidents)
    - a spike that's high but could be chance
    
    A live check on the current data finds Broadview North.
41. **No score changes.** For the same stored data, neighbourhood and street scores are identical before and after this change. A test stores a property-crime story and a police pattern next to a cell and checks that its score doesn't change.
42. **Web:**
    - the section shows Trending, Recent by type and Patterns in police reports, each labelled with its window or data date
    - a pattern opens its neighbourhood
    - the neighbourhood panel shows its own pattern
    - no console errors, and no sideways scrolling at phone width

## Design

### Data
- **Migration 010:**
  - `news_events.category` also allows `property_crime`
  - new sources `news_citynews`, `news_global` (and `news_star` if approved), each with its licence note
  - new table `crime_patterns` (neighbourhood, offence group, recent count, usual per 30 days, first and last date, chance, police data date, `computed_at`)
- **Feeds:** `sources/news.py` gets one parser for all RSS feeds (the CBC parser already reads standard RSS) and a list of feeds with their publishers. The tests showed CityNews, Global and the Star all parse with it.
- **Classification:**
  - order: violent, then protest, then property crime
  - new negative patterns: sports-result wording, "memorial", and an earlier year mentioned as the time of the event
  - property words: break-in, break and enter, burglary, theft, stolen, catalytic converter, carjacking (stays violent), arson, vandalism

### Windows by type
- **Where they live:** `scoring/news.py` gets `DISPLAY_WINDOWS = {"protest": 2 days, "violent_incident": 7 days, "property_crime": 60 days}`.
- **Scoring:** `news_score()` still uses only protest and violent incidents within 24 hours, so its result is unchanged by design.

### Trending (`scoring/trending.py`, pure functions)
- **Words:** each headline is reduced to significant words: lower case, simple plurals removed, and common words and "Toronto", "police" and "man" dropped.
- **Grouping:** two stories go together when they share at least 2 significant words and are within 3 days of each other. Groups are joined transitively, using a small union-find.
- **Trending:** a group with at least 3 stories from at least 2 publishers. Groups are ordered by number of stories, then by newest.
- **Where it's computed:** at request time from the last 7 days of stored stories. That's at most a few hundred headlines, so it takes milliseconds and needs no new table.

### Patterns (`scoring/patterns.py`, pure functions; stored by `build-scores`)
- **Offences:** break-ins and auto thefts (`break_and_enter`, `motor_vehicle_theft`).
- **Recent:** the latest 30 days of police data.
- **Usual:** incidents in the 12 months before that, divided by 12, with a floor of 0.5 so a quiet area can't divide by nearly nothing.
- **Pattern when:** recent ≥ 5, recent ≥ 2 × usual, and the Poisson chance of at least that many is below 1%.
- **Excluded places:** jail incidents stay excluded, as for scores.

### API (added only; `/api/v1` stays compatible)
- **`GET /api/v1/news-events?window=by_type`:** each item gains `window_days`. The existing `since_hours` behaviour is unchanged.
- **`GET /api/v1/news-trending`:** `[{headline, url, publisher, published_at, story_count, outlet_count, first_published_at, category, stories: [...]}]`
- **`GET /api/v1/crime-patterns`:** `[{neighbourhood_id, neighbourhood, offence, recent, usual_per_30_days, times_usual, first_date, last_date, data_as_of}]`
- **`/neighbourhoods/{id}`:** gains `patterns` (that neighbourhood's, possibly empty).

### Web
- **"Recent news" becomes "News and patterns"** (unverified tag on the news parts):
  - **Trending:** up to 3 groups. Each has the headline as a link, then "4 stories from 3 outlets since Oct 13". The other stories are in a collapsed list.
  - **Recent:** three short lists, "Protests (last 2 days)", "Violent incidents (last 7 days)" and "Property crime (last 60 days)". Each shows up to 5 stories, with "Show all" when there are more. An empty list says so.
  - **Patterns in police reports:** each line is clickable and opens the neighbourhood. Underneath: "Police data up to Oct 1; reported incidents only; not a prediction."
- **Neighbourhood panel:** a "Pattern in police reports" line under the reasons, when there is one.

### Review checklist

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Edge cases | **Feeds:** a feed that is down or malformed is skipped and recorded as failed. **Duplicate URLs** across feeds are stored once (the `url` is unique). **Same story, different URL** is grouped by trending, not deduplicated. **Empty lists** say so. **Police data** that's old or missing shows its date or nothing. |
| Security | **Feeds:** feed text is untrusted: only headline, summary, link and time are kept, links stay limited to http(s), and text is inserted with `textContent` as today. **New endpoints:** read-only with no parameters, except the validated `window`. |
| Performance | **Live ingest:** 2–3 more small feeds per hourly run, about 20–80 KB each. **Trending:** computed over at most a few hundred headlines. **Patterns:** one grouped query per daily build. |
| Licensing | **CityNews and Global:** personal, non-commercial, the same as CBC; fine for the internal demo, and recorded in the sources. **Toronto Star:** only with your approval (decision 1). **Before a public launch:** a licensed news source is needed, as already noted. |
| Fairness | **Patterns compare each area with itself,** so a usually busy area isn't flagged for being busy. **The 1% chance test** keeps false alarms to about 1–2 across the city. |

### Alternatives considered
1. **Same window for every type** (e.g. a week): a 2-month break-in series would vanish after a week, and protests would linger 5 days after they end. Rejected.
2. **Patterns from news only:** news is patchy and gives no counts, while police data has every report. News is still shown alongside.
3. **Trending by counting keywords across all news:** keyword counts like "police" or "Toronto" rise for no reason. Grouping by shared words between headlines finds actual events. Chosen.
4. **Raising scores for patterns:** you said no.

### Risks

| Risk | How it's reduced |
|---|---|
| Keyword classification still makes mistakes | The fixes above cover the real mistakes found, the list stays labelled unverified, and the classifier tests use real headlines. |
| Grouping joins unrelated stories that share 2 words (e.g. "Bloor" and "shooting") | Groups also need to be within 3 days. A test checks that today's two big events don't merge. If you see bad groups, the threshold can go up to 3 shared words. |
| Police data is about two weeks behind | Every pattern shows its police data date. |
| Neighbourhood boundaries split a series (Broadview North and Playter Estates-Danforth are next to each other, and both appear) | Both are shown. Street-level patterns are a follow-up. |
| News feeds change their address or format | Each feed is checked independently, and failures appear in "Data collected". |
