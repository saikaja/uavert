# Phases 1–3 (addition), revision 2: Odds alongside the score: leave out incidents inside the two jails

**Task folder:** docs/work/2026-10-01-backend-foundation/  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-07  ·  **Status:** Approved  ·  **Revises:** [01-03-odds.md](01-03-odds.md)

## Why this revision
Checking the odds on real data (build step O2) showed that incidents recorded inside Toronto's two jails are counted as neighbourhood crime:

| Place | Neighbourhood | Incidents at the jail's point (premises "Other") | Of which in 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toronto South Detention Centre, 130 Horner Ave | Mimico-Queensway | 1,191 (almost all assaults, many on officers) | 824 |
| Toronto East Detention Centre, 55 Civic Rd | Clairlea-Birchmount | 238 (all assaults) | 170 |

This made Mimico-Queensway's assault odds "1 in 28", 7 times the Toronto figure, when nearly all of those assaults happened inside the jail. It has also always raised both neighbourhoods' risk scores. Sai chose (in chat, 2026-10-07): **"Leave out of odds and score"**. Because this changes the risk score, it needs its own approval.

## What was done
- Located the incidents: Toronto Police places each jail's incidents at a single point (43.61208, -79.51581 and 43.72794, -79.28276). Within 300 m, only 1 other "Other" incident exists at each (177 m and 262 m away), so a 50 m radius captures the jail and nothing else.
- Dry-ran the scoring with and without them on the live data (nothing written):

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Mimico-Queensway score | 40 Moderate | 24 Lower reported risk |
| Clairlea-Birchmount score | 28 Moderate | 24 Lower reported risk |
| Other neighbourhood scores | | 37 go up by 1 point (the typical neighbourhood shifts slightly); the rest are unchanged |
| Street scores | | unchanged (they already count only outdoor, transit and business incidents) |
| Mimico-Queensway odds (high / medium / low / any) | 1 in 320 / 28 / 190 / 23 | 1 in 1,000 / 310 / 200 / 110 |
| Clairlea-Birchmount odds | 1 in 510 / 100 / 120 / 51 | 1 in 620 / 220 / 120 / 70 |
| Toronto odds | 1 in 470 / 210 / 140 / 71 | 1 in 470 / 220 / 140 / 73 |

## Change to the definition
New acceptance criteria:
7. Incidents with premises "Other" within 50 m of either jail are left out of every crime score and the odds. Incidents outside, in businesses or on transit near the jails, and the homicide data (no premises type), still count.
8. The rules page lists the places left out and why.
9. After the rebuild, Mimico-Queensway scores 24 and Clairlea-Birchmount 24 (give or take 1 point if new data arrives before the rebuild).

## Change to the design
- A new reference file, `data/excluded_places.csv`: name, address, longitude, latitude, radius (50 m), the premises type left out ("Other"), the reason, and the date checked. It sits with the other reference data, so another institution can be added later without code changes.
- `load_events` reads each incident's location and drops those that match an excluded place, so the neighbourhood score, its reasons, the odds and the street scores all see the same events.
- The rules page gets a line under "Limitations" naming the two jails.

**Not included (follow-up):** other institutions where many incidents are recorded at one address, such as hospitals and shelters. The check found no other single point near this size: the next largest are a transit station at 93 assaults and an apartment building at 47. Those are places the public goes to, so they stay in.
